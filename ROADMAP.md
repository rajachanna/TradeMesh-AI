# Roadmap

## Implemented: v0.1 paper MVP

- Webhook-driven paper simulation for BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT and SOLUSDT
- Cash and symbol exposure limits
- Duplicate order-id prevention and SQLite persistence
- Authenticated portfolio API, read-only dashboard and tests

## Planned (not yet implemented)

- TradingView alert templates and signed/freshness-checked webhook signals
- IBKR paper trading adapter and MT5 demo adapter
- CEX sandbox connectors; DEX quote/swap simulation
- Expanded dashboard, slippage/fee models, backtesting and AI analytics
- Production-grade risk controls, authentication, audit logging and security review

No live orders, broker connections, profitability claims or real market prices are provided in v0.1.
